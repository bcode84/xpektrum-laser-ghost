Bulk Order FAQ

Technical specifications and logistical details for our commercial partners. Everything you need to know about our laser engraving workflow.

Our standard turnaround for B2B orders is 3-5 business days. For high-volume industrial runs or complex custom signage, we provide a specific timeline during the quoting process to ensure your deadlines are met with precision.

While we specialize in commercial volume, we don't enforce a strict MOQ for all items. However, our pricing is optimized for bulk production. For industrial tags and corporate gifts, orders typically start at 25 units to remain cost-effective.

Yes, we ship daily across Quebec and the rest of Canada. We use reliable commercial carriers to ensure your branded products, hospitality items, or industrial labels arrive safely and on schedule at your facility or event venue.

For maximum precision, we prefer vector files such as .AI, .EPS, .SVG, or .DXF. If you only have high-resolution raster files (.PNG, .JPG), our design team can often convert them for a small technical preparation fee.

Absolutely. We frequently work with client-supplied materials, from restaurant glassware to industrial metal plates. We perform a material compatibility test to ensure our 80W CO₂ laser delivers the high-contrast, permanent finish you expect.
