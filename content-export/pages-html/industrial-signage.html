Technical Specifications

Precision-engineered capabilities for B2B requirements. Our xTool P3 80W CO₂ infrastructure is built for speed, accuracy, and industrial-grade reliability.

Maximum Bed Size

600 x 400 mm Working Area

Our xTool P3 80W CO₂ laser features a generous workspace designed for high-volume commercial production. Whether you are manufacturing large-scale architectural signage or batch-processing corporate gifts, our equipment handles oversized materials with industrial precision and zero compromise on edge quality.

Tech-Note

Material Optimization

For large orders, we utilize nesting software to maximize material yield on our 600x400mm bed, significantly reducing waste and cost for your project.

Supported Formats

.DXF, .SVG, .AI Compatibility

We integrate seamlessly with your design workflow. Our systems are optimized for industry-standard vector formats, ensuring that your technical drawings and brand assets are translated into physical products with mathematical accuracy. We handle complex geometries and intricate typography with absolute fidelity to your original files.

Tech-Note

Vector Preparation

Ensure all fonts are converted to outlines and lines are set to 'hairline' width in your .AI or .DXF files for the fastest processing and most accurate quote.

Material Thickness

Up to 15mm Cutting Depth

Engineered for versatility, our 80W CO₂ beam penetrates materials up to 15mm thick. From robust acrylic panels for lobby branding to thick hardwood for hospitality displays, we deliver clean, vertical cuts that require no secondary finishing. This power ensures fast turnaround times even on heavy-duty industrial substrates.

Tech-Note

Edge Quality

Thicker acrylics (10mm+) result in a 'flame-polished' edge directly from the laser, providing a premium glass-like finish without manual buffing.
